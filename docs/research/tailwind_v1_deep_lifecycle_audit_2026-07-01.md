## Executive Summary

The deployable book under audit is **TAILWIND-v1** — the linear TSMOM + BAB fund-of-sleeves that is next to capital. Its **correctness core is genuinely strong**: the causal signal machinery (dual `assert_causal` future-bar + current-bar tripwires firing at load, the exact X2/sg1-btc blind spot), the DSR formula (MATH-R01, golden mutation-tripwire green), debt-free short accounting, byte-faithful real-env parity harness, honest non-zero slippage, the deflated-Sharpe statistic, and the bundle SHA chain are all above-median practice and correctly close prior-audit gaps. Capital is currently and correctly **BLOCKED** (DSR 0.918 < 0.95; `deploy_gating: false`), so nothing here is an emergency — the findings are about the *next* promotion.

**The dominant cross-cutting fault — the pre-capital gate infrastructure grades the WRONG BOOK and does not enforce the one control that already fails.** Every Stage-3/4/benchmark artifact the TAILWIND capital gate points at was built for a *different composition* (momentum + **rates-carry**), which `tailwind_v1.yaml` explicitly EXCLUDES as duration beta. The only adversarial pre-capital audit (`audit_two_sleeve_book.py`), the DSR that "blocks," the `dsr_n_trials` count, the stress-leverage bound, and the fixed-lot stress pass are all mis-scoped, un-forked, or un-wired for the momentum+BAB book TAILWIND actually trades (P7-01, P7-02, P7-06, P11-01..05). And where the correct statistic *is* computed on the right book — Deflated-Sharpe — it FAILS (0.918 across the whole N-bracket) yet the audit script classifies the miss as S2 and emits `decision: PROCEED_no_S1`; no runner or validator consumes the verdict, so a `deploy_gating` flip inherits **zero enforced haircut** (P11-02). The honest 0.918<0.95 block today is a human reading a JSON, not a wired gate.

**Top S1/S2 themes (21 confirmed S1/S2 findings — 2 S1, 19 S2):**
1. **Stale/wrong-artifact deploy verdicts on the legacy RL prop-firm path (the only two S1s).** The standalone GMGP1-BTC Stage-4 verdict grades a RETIRED WF-fold seed-456, not the deployed L1-multiseed seed-123 (P8-01), and the deployed recent-OOS eval models **zero slippage** on a 4440-trade/96-day 15m book, producing a +5437% fee-only PASS (P8-03). Contrast sg1-btc-deployed, which applies 5bps and lands RETRAIN.
2. **Wrong-book / un-wired pre-capital gates for TAILWIND (the dominant fault above).** Tier-2 audit, DSR, stress sub-report, and PBO are all momentum+rates or research-path only (P7-01, P11-01, P11-02).
3. **Ensemble weight/selection leakage on the RL path.** `ens_pf_weighted` weights are TEST-window PFs identical to the re-scored Stage-2.5 window (P9-01/P9-05), and the swap-bundle writer never persists `seed_pfs`, so a promoted `ens_pf_weighted` bundle silently collapses to equal-weight live (P9-02) — with a provenance stamp that is falsely `diversity_aware` (P9-04).
4. **Documented safeguards with no code consumer.** Training-health kill gates (P5-01) and their input metrics never computed; the [15,40] multiplicity gate silently no-ops for every non-crypto/non-allocator prop-firm asset (P5-03); the mid-interval sim↔live parity boundary — where the X2 leak lived — is UNTESTED (P2-01); the always-on risk kills are uncalibrated placeholders that the composition-match cross-check falsely PASSes (P10-04).

**Credit where due (S4 positives):** the load-time dual `assert_causal` tripwires (P1-07, P3-04), mid-window delisting/zero-price protection (P1-08), causal freeze-and-restart EMA-Z (P2-06), correct T+1 step ordering with echo-policy tripwires (P3-01), debt-free symmetric short accounting (P3-02), honest participation-slippage fill model mirrored line-for-line into the real-env parity harness (P3-03/P3-05/P10-07), the DSR formula + golden tripwire (P4-01/P4-02) net-of-cost DSR input (P4-05), BUG-01/BUG-03 enforcement (P5-07/P5-08), the pre-committed CV-ambiguous escalation band and workstream-agnostic launcher (P6-07/P6-08), correct chosen-rule single-source and val-isolated selection (P9-08/P9-09/P9-10), the SHA bundle fail-closed chain (P10-08), and the correctly-implemented deflated-Sharpe / PSR-MinTRL / block-bootstrap battery (P11-09/P11-10/P11-11).

**Verdict:** TAILWIND-v1 is engineered soundly at the waterline but its **capital-promotion evidence does not certify the book it will trade**. Before any capital: fork the Tier-2 audit + DSR + stress onto the momentum+BAB composition, make the DSR verdict structurally blocking, and build a TAILWIND-native Stage-4 OOS artifact with honest cost. Separately, the legacy RL prop-firm verdicts (GMGP1-BTC) carry two live-facing S1 staleness/frictionless defects that should be regenerated.
