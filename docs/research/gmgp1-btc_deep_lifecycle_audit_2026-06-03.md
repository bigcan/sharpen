## Executive Summary

The pipeline is **rigorous below the waterline and porous above it.** The low-level correctness machinery is genuinely strong and independently re-verified this session — the V7 X2 coarse-bar de-leak is correct and tripwire-guarded (P2-01/P2-02/P3-01), the DSR formula matches MATH-R01 to 1e-9 with a mutation tripwire (P4-01), short accounting is symmetric and debt-free (P3-09), LEAK-1 EMA-Z isolation is wired end-to-end (P2-10), bundle SHA256 integrity is all-or-nothing and fail-closed (P10-09), and the block-bootstrap / externalized gate YAMLs are above academic median (P11-06). But the **decision-and-validation layer that is supposed to certify robustness is built on stale, leak-era, frictionless artifacts and on safeguards that are declared in the protocol/configs but have no Python consumer.**

**The single dominant cross-cutting fault — leak-era + frictionless artifacts read as deploy-gating verdicts.** Every deploy-gating evidence object for the headline strategy (gmgp1-btc) predates the X2 de-leak fix (commit `6c027e19`, 2026-05-30) and/or runs the falsified zero-slippage cost model, yet still sits on disk as a green gate.
