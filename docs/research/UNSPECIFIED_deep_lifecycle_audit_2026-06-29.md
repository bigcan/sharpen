## Executive Summary

The pipeline is **strong at the correctness waterline and weak at the cost/decision/wiring layer above it.** The low-level machinery is genuinely sound and tripwire-protected — the X2 coarse-bar causal map (P1-08, P2-05), LEAK-1 EMA-Z split isolation (P1-09, P2-07), the DSR formula with its numeric mutation tripwire (P3-09, P4-05/06), debt-free SHORT accounting (P3-08), the dual-equity mid-mark shadow (P3-10), the bundle SHA chain (P10-10), and the deflated signal-eval funnel with purged CPCV / PSR / MinTRL (P11-03/04/05/09). But the *gates that are supposed to certify a strategy before it touches capital* are repeatedly **declared-but-not-wired**, and the entire eval/selection chain is run under an **understated cost model**.

**The single dominant cross-cutting fault — the frictionless / cost-understated evaluation regime.** Slippage defaults to `0.0` and is left unset across every deploy-gating path: the gold backtest (P3-01), the WF primary verdict (P7-02), the Stage-4 recent-OOS verdict (P8-03, an **S1** — 5437% / 14431% returns with sub-2.3% DD), the live cost-drift detector's denominator (P10-01), and the retrain OOS gate (P10-08). The same omission propagates a *frictionless artifact* through every stage exactly as the AlphaSeek / VWAP-gold / MACD-scalp post-mortems warned. It compounds with the **wrong-checkpoint / in-sample Stage-4 verdict** (P8-01, P8-02 — the gating OOS artifact tests WF seed-456 fold-3, not the live L1 seed-123, on a window that overlaps that fold's own train+val), producing a capital-gating verdict that is simultaneously off-policy, partly in-sample, and frictionless. The sister fix for this exact class was already applied to sg1-btc (slippage 5.0 + live-ckpt re-point) and **never carried to this workstream.**

**Top S1/S2 themes:**
1. **Cost honesty is absent at the gates.** No slippage in the OOS/WF/retrain cost models; the live cost-drift denominator is permanently mis-scaled (P3-01, P7-02, P8-03, P10-01, P10-08).
2. **Stage-4 OOS verdict is structurally invalid.** Wrong seed/checkpoint (P8-01), in-sample window overlap (P8-02), no WF-median baseline (P8-04), no absurd-return tripwire (P8-05), and it bypasses the staged manifest contract (P8-07).
3. **Ensemble weights leak and silently collapse.** `ens_pf_weighted` weights are TEST-split-derived on the same window the rule is re-scored (P9-01, S1) and are never written into the live manifest, so the PROMOTEd rule silently degrades to equal-weight `ens_mean` (P9-02, S1).
4. **Declared-but-not-wired safeguards at five+ stages.** DATA-CLEAN stale-detector not in the manifest gate (P1-02, S1), training-health kill gates with zero consumers (P5-01), the [15,40] multiplicity check skipped for the entire FX/gold/futures fleet (P5-02), no generic L1 gate evaluator (P6-01), the PF-XCHECK halt set to report-only (P3-03), and `validate_config` not auto-invoked by any runner (P5-03).
5. **The highest-value missing overfitting control is PBO/CSCV** (P11-01) — the only Bailey-LdP statistic with no in-repo primitive — on an otherwise disciplined deflated stack; the staged WF allocator verdict carries no multiplicity haircut (P11-02).

**Credit where due (S4 positives):** the X2 causal coarse-bar map dual-guarded by counterfactual tripwires, LEAK-1 EMA-Z warmup-carry isolation, the DSR golden-value mutation tripwire, debt-free SHORT accounting, the non-perturbing dual-equity shadow, the bundle SHA chain, BUG-01/03 honoring, the reward↔PF Spearman diagnostic, the workstream-agnostic L1 launcher, the pre-committed CV escalation band, the frozen-N DSR commitment device, PSR/MinTRL at the correct per-period convention, and purged/embargoed CPCV — all real and to be preserved.

**Verdict:** Capital is structurally protected from blow-up by the in-engine integrity/drift machinery, but **no deploy-gating verdict in this workstream can currently be trusted** until (a) honest cost (taker + slippage) is set across the OOS/WF/retrain configs and the selection is re-derived, (b) the Stage-4 OOS verdict is re-run on the live checkpoint over a strictly-OOS window, and (c) the `ens_pf_weighted` weight source/leak and manifest-collapse are fixed. These are about the *next promotion*, not an active blow-up.
