## Executive Summary

TAILWIND-v1's signal layer is causal and guarded by tripwires with real teeth. The research and executor price caches agree bar for bar. The executor's accounting (SHORT-ACCT, cost-once, participation) is correct. Every recorded number reproduces exactly offline. What fails is the step from those numbers to a deploy decision.

**The dominant cross-cutting fault is that the certified book is an idealized one, not the book that would trade.** The executor DSR 0.967 and P(pass) 0.667 turned TAILWIND to PASS on 2026-09-29. Both are computed on an object no account can hold:
- the unfinanced total return of a book that is persistently net long (+0.50-0.55x) and about 2.2x gross;
- filled at a month-end close that no code on the path can schedule;
- held as fixed-notional labels that are never re-marked;
- scored against firm limits only, close to close, with the declared kills switched off.

Remove any one of these idealizations and both numbers fall below their gates (Verdict 2). The largest and best-attested one is the omitted cost of carry. It is the only S1 that four pillars (T3, T4, T5, T7) found and reproduced independently. Charging just the T-bill rate on net exposure takes executor DSR to 0.907-0.934, below 0.95 at every N in the declared bracket, and P(pass) to 0.632.

This is the 2026-08-18 finding ("the book that was certified is not the book that would trade"). The executor recompute closed it on the mechanics axis; it is now re-opened on the financing, timing and order-semantics axes. It survived because of a second, enabling fault that every TAILWIND Tier-2 has found: no capital gate on this path can fire mechanically. Each script writes JSON and exits 0. The soak and validator gates are tautological, vacuous, or crash.

### Verdict (1): Is the R-3 decision-lead fix correct and causal?

**Yes, as a backtest timing: it is correct, causal and fail-closed. No, as a deploy fact: no code can execute it, no gate can observe it, and its gain is within calendar noise.**

What is proven, independently in every pillar:
- **Causality at every row it reads.** At step k the lead reads conviction and vol from row k+1. Those rows are built from TSMOM closes ≤ k−4, BAB closes ≤ k and vol returns ≤ k. The env reads vol before the clock advances and fills at price[k+1]. Alpha uses shift(1) and keys on fill stamps. Sources: `sharpen/features/cross_asset_signals.py:57-58, :64-67`; `sharpen/features/defensive_signals.py:63`; `sharpen/envs/multi_asset_allocator_env.py:237-249, :293-299, :394`; `sharpen/envs/allocator_factory.py:305, :309, :560`; `sharpen/paper/two_sleeve.py:127`. See T2-16, T4-15, T6-20, T7-19.
- **Fail-closed.** The builders declare their cutoff lags. rates_carry declares 0 and is refused. The final bar is never acted on. The RL gate baseline refuses the lead. Sources: `sharpen/data/cross_asset_loader.py:493-494, :649-650, :705-706`; `allocator_factory.py:292-299, :306, :373, :378-388, :415`.
- **Tests with teeth.** Two independent mutant sets (17 mutants) were all killed and the controls pass (T2-17). 189 related tests pass.
- **Real-data perturbation.** Two perturbations were applied: crashing the fill bar, and bumping every later bar. Neither changed any decision at or before f−1. This held at 58 decision bars (T2-11), and through the full `sim_oracle` path at 2008-10-31, 2020-03-31 and 2022-06-30 (T5-14).
- **Statistical signature.** The +0.081 SR mirrors the research book's own −0.072 one-day-delay cost. With the gross cap, slippage and dust filter off, the fixed executor (0.7269) sits 0.0055 below the research basis (0.7324) on the same window. A look-ahead would push it above (T3-07, T3-14).
- **Exact reproduction.** SR 0.5541 → 0.6351, DSR 0.9296 → 0.9671, P(pass) 16/26 → 14/21 (T1-15, T5-13).

What is not realized:
- **The documented live target cannot execute it.** Month-end flags are the group-max of the window's own stamps (`allocator_factory.py:144-150`; `sharpen/paper/parity_harness.py:361-372`). The final row is held (`allocator_factory.py:306`). So `w[-1]`, which `allocator_factory.py:437-442` names "the live order-generation target", first carries a new month's conviction after close d+1 and fills at d+2. A placeholder bar gives d+1. Forward-prefix consistency breaks at 99.2% of real month-ends (median L1 0.59 against the 0.05 parity gate). See T2-01, T1-04, T4-02, T6-01, T7-03.
- **It is executable in principle.** At close d−1, append the next two calendar sessions and read w[−2]. This reproduced the batch weights exactly in 9 of 9 cases (T4 skeptic). It needs an ex-ante NYSE calendar. The repo has only a CME 2026 holiday list (`sharpen/futures/live/cme_calendar.py`) and a USFederalHolidayCalendar (`sharpen/crucible/data/cftc_cot.py`). Only 5 of 247 months need holiday awareness: Memorial Day 2010 and 2021, and Good Friday 2013, 2018 and 2024 (T6-01).
- **Alpha is not held on the final row.** Forward alpha drifts past 0.05 on 7.3% of mid-month steps at lead 1 and 6.4% at lead 0. So falling back to lead 0 does not restore forward parity (T2-08).
- **No gate can see it.** Parity is exactly 0 under both leads (T2-02, T6-03). missed_rebalances read 0 while every interior month-end switch filled one close late before the fix (T2-07).
- **The gain is fragile.** 0.081 SR is about one rebalance-offset standard deviation (0.042) on the research basis, and the delay curve is non-monotone: d 0.635, d+1 0.554, d+2 0.573 (T3-04, T7-10, T4-02).
- **It is unpinned.** `decision_lead_bars` defaults to 0 (fail-open), no test pins either config, and the change is uncommitted (T6-15, T6-08).
