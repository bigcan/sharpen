## Executive Summary

The screening machinery is sound and reproducible. Four independent reconstructions reproduce P1 (all five cards), P2 (all 200 placebo draws) and P3 bit-exactly from committed code. No look-ahead was found, the clean-window firewall held, and every P1-P3 rule was in git before any full-corpus score existed. What is not sound is everything between that state and the single look. The P4 leg, its sentinel protocol and its price panel do not exist yet, nothing pins the instrument that will produce the clean scores, and the statistics that licensed the look are weaker than they read.

**Dominant cross-cutting risk: the look is irreversible, but nothing pins it to what was screened.** Six layers can change between screening and P4, and each change would pass every existing check:

- **Model.** `jev.model` is the alias `jev-latest`, and the only guard fires when two versions appear within one run. A clean run served wholly by a newer model completes normally, and a response without a `model` field is recorded as the alias (reproduced offline). Six of seven pillars found this independently; J4-03 and J7-01 hold it at S1.
- **Text.** `pipeline_sha` hashes `anonymize.py` and `corpus.py` only, and is compared with the running code, not with the screening stamp. `html_to_text`, bs4 and lxml are unpinned, and the EDGAR cache stores already-extracted text.
- **Prices.** No P4 panel spec exists. Screening closes are yfinance total return, Alpaca's default is raw, and the repo's only Alpaca precedent is split-only. DATA-CLEAN cannot see a close-to-close corporate-action error; the screening panel already carries the DHR +61% spin-off jump on active rows.
- **Universe.** PIT membership, constituents and `company_tickers.json` are unhashed, and the planned refresh overwrites them.
- **Code.** Construction and evaluation modules are unstamped, and every leg ran on a dirty tree in a checkout that other sessions switch.
- **Protocol.** No code writes `OPENED.json`. The guard accepts any JSON with an `opened_utc` key, and ADR-3 both requires and forbids the sentinel's existence during the build.

This dominates because it is the only risk class that corrupts the look rather than weakens it, and it cannot be repaired afterwards. It also carries the clean window's validity. The best evidence that the window is clean is that jev-1.13.0 cannot date masked 2025 releases: 1 of 27 correct, 0 of 15 in 2025H1, while it still names 96% of the filers (refuted J2-01). That evidence holds for jev-1.13.0 only.

**Supporting themes**

1. **The PROMISING verdict that licenses the look depends on the estimator (RC-B).** The funnel corrects overlapping 5-day labels with Bartlett weights at lag h−1, which under-weights the MA(4) overlap (IC autocorrelations 0.78/0.56/0.37/0.19). Every overlap-consistent t falls below the frozen 3.0 bar: Hansen-Hodrick 2.66, Newey-West with automatic bandwidth 2.90, non-overlapping subsamples 2.20-2.89 (mean 2.60), bootstrap-implied 2.71. Under any of them K3 fires. DSR (0.946 → 0.908) and FDR still pass. The verdict is rule-compliant, because the prereg binds P1 to the frozen funnel. But this is a latent defect in unchanged, audited funnel code (v14.0) that touches every h > 1 Crucible verdict, and it must be disclosed and decided before the look.
2. **P4 has about a third of its registered power (RC-C).** The "5/8" figure came from planted signals about 1.8× steadier per unit IC than the candidate (P1 t 5.0-8.2 at IC 0.010, against 3.21 at 0.009). Replaying the exact P4 recipe on 137 screening windows passes 24-25% of the time. Windows starting in 2018 or later pass 3 of 65, and none of 24 since mid-2021. These are upper bounds. A fail would carry a likelihood ratio of about 0.8 (nearly uninformative), a pass about 6-10. Waiting does not help, because 50% power needs about five years of clean data.
3. **A pass would certify a rank statistic, not a book (RC-D).** The same weights earn Sharpe 0.76 against return ranks but 0.02 against returns. The frictionless Sharpe is 0.02 ± 0.28 and changes sign with the rebalance phase. The book leg is nearly collinear with the IC leg (corr 0.97) and never binds, and P5 at 63 bars has a Sharpe SE of about 2.0. Break-even cost is about 0.7 bp one-way. A pass still says something about the IC: in every screening window with t ≥ 2 the net book was positive (median 0.60), so the claim that a pass is worthless (J6-14) was refuted.
4. **The mechanism is weaker than registered (RC-J).** P2's same-week firm shuffle cannot separate a persistent firm trait from event-timed news. Last quarter's score keeps 90% of the IC, and the IC is about zero at the release and lives at ages 21-62. Momentum, reversal and announcement-return controls cut the P3 coefficient by about 42% (NW t 2.33). E3's judgment carries 84% of the standalone IC. Within screening, memory is neither implicated nor excluded.
5. **Declared but not wired:** the P4 leg and its promised refusal tests, the C5 leak detector, `status == PASS` (the artifacts say COMPLETE), `edgar_filings_sha`, `min_adv_usd`, size neutralization, the `decile_monotonic` caveat and the DATA-CLEAN status.

**How much to trust the screening result: trust the computation, not the edge.** The numbers are exactly what the registered code produces, and no leak was found. As evidence, however, the result is weak:
- It is an upper bound by construction: Jev names 93.5% of masked filers.
- It passes three legs by thin margins. t ≥ 3 holds only under the Bartlett estimator; the minimum subperiod IC-IR of 0.0097 holds only with four splits; the frictionless Sharpe is positive in only four of five rebalance phases.
- It is concentrated in 2012-2017. The 2018-2024 IC is 0.0045 (t 1.1).
- It is rank-only: the dollar Sharpe is 0.02 ± 0.28 gross and −0.28 net.
- About 40% of it is shared with price signals.

Read it as a marginal, regime-concentrated rank association that survived its registered gates, not as evidence of a tradeable or LLM-specific edge. Even if it is real, P(P4 pass) is about 0.2-0.25.
