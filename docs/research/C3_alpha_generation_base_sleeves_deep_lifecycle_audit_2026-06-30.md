## Executive Summary

**GATE QUESTION — Can a `--mode real` generation survivor be TRUSTED as a research lead yet?**
**NO — TRUST-BLOCKED.** A survivor cannot be read as a research lead until the fitness/deflation gate is repaired, because the gate that is supposed to make "0 PROMISING over 4,495 genomes" meaningful is itself **mis-calibrated in both directions at once** — simultaneously *passable by a redundant rediscovery* and *near-unreachable by a genuine marginal diversifier* — and there is **no positive control** proving it can ever discriminate. The advisory cap (PROMISING ≤ ceiling, no auto-promotion) and the one-basis cost accounting are genuinely sound (S4 credits), so nothing is at capital risk today; the defect is that the loop's headline result is **unfalsifiable as a filter-quality claim**.

**THE SINGLE DOMINANT FAULT — the fitness/deflation gate is not the multiplicity control it advertises.** Three compounding defects converge on the same PROMISING decision:
1. **GP4-01 (S1):** the shipped C1 combiner is pure inverse-vol with `tilt_strength=0` and **no collinearity term**, so a candidate ~94% correlated with the strongest base sleeve earns a *positive* ΔSharpe by concentration — a redundant *rediscovery* of the existing edge can pass. The fitness docstring's "redundancy earns ≈0" invariant is **false** for the shipped combiner.
2. **GP4-02 / GP6-03 / GP7-02 (S2/S3):** `deflated_sharpe_ratio` is fed the **within-book CPCV path Sharpes of one augmented book** as `trial_sharpes`, not the cross-search dispersion of the file-drawer N. The DSR core formula is itself a faithful Bailey/López-de-Prado implementation (GP4-07 ✓) — the bug is in *what is fed to it*. The 4,495-trial correction enters only through the order-statistic multiplier on a wrong (within-strategy) variance.
3. **GP4-03 / GP7-01 / GP7-04 (S2):** the gate is over-determined against the very diversifiers it exists to surface — the standalone HLZ t≥3 hurdle implies an annSR floor (~0.84–2.2 depending on split) that **both validated base sleeves would fail**, and the absolute-book DSR≥0.90 bar is erratic/near-unreachable for plausible cost-netted cross-asset alphas.

**Top S1/S2 themes:**
- **T1 — Gate cannot discriminate (GP4-01, GP4-03, GP4-04, GP7-01, GP7-03):** collinear-passable + diversifier-rejecting + no positive control + no PBO/CSCV → "0 PROMISING" is consistent with "gate never fires."
- **T2 — Deflation fed the wrong dispersion pool (GP4-02, GP6-03, GP7-02):** the file-drawer N multiplicity correction is structurally weakened; the deferred M1 "population dispersion pool" is load-bearing, not a refinement.
- **T3 — DATA-CLEAN invariant bypassed on the panel feeding every candidate (GP1-02):** the panel is cleaned in-memory only; no `.bak`, no manifest, no EARNED status, the gmgp1-gold stale-print FAIL gate is discarded.

**What must change FIRST (before any survivor is read):** (a) add an orthogonality/collinearity guard to the fitness or ship the ADR-C1-5 correlation down-weight (GP4-01); (b) feed DSR the population dispersion of the search, not one book's CPCV paths (GP4-02); (c) add a **planted-diversifier positive control** that MUST yield ≥1 PROMISING (GP4-04) — until it passes, treat "0 PROMISING" as "gate never fired," not "no alpha." Route the panel through `fetch_and_clean` so DATA-CLEAN is honored (GP1-02). Everything downstream of these is documentation-honesty and robustness hardening.

**Refutation count:** 1 finding refuted across all pillars (GP5-04, the "0 exceptions over 10,000 genomes" claim — 42 `cap`-token ValueErrors found, but run-level crash-proofing via try/except holds, so benign).
