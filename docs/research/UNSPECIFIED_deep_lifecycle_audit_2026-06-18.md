## Executive Summary

The codebase audited here spans **two distinct surfaces** evaluated under one lifecycle lens: the **V7/SAC directional engine** (gmgp1/sg1 — the canary the cost-corrected falsification ran on, already shelved) and the **cross-asset momentum allocator** (xsec_momentum / `multi_asset_allocator_env` — the strategy actually heading to capital). The low-level correctness machinery is genuinely strong and verified-by-mutation: the DSR formula (MATH-R01), LEAK-1 EMA-Z per-split isolation, the X2 coarse-bar causal map (closed at mid-interval truncation, not just end-of-data), SHORT-ACCT debt-free symmetry, single-source fee accounting, the v2.3 bundle SHA chain, and the paired stationary block-bootstrap all hold exactly under adversarial perturbation. The leak that erased the sg1-btc edge is genuinely closed.

**The single dominant cross-cutting fault: declared-but-not-wired safeguards on the decision/validation layer.** Across P1, P3, P5, P6, P7, P8, P9, and P10, the protocol/config text repeatedly advertises a gate, kill-trigger, recency check, or selection mechanism that **no Python consumer ever reads** — and in several cases the evidence the gate would need is never even persisted. This is the exact pattern that hid the X2 leak through hundreds of green audits. Concretely: training-health kill gates (`entropy_floor`/`q_div_max`/`action_sat_max`) are logged-only (P5-01); the HPO PF floor (`hpo_pf_floor`) has zero consumer (P5-02); the multiplicity `[15,40]` check is bypassed for the allocator's actual full-train budget (P5-03); DATA-CLEAN's §3 rejection rules are skipped entirely for `yfinance*` (P1-01) and the runtime clean is self-certified with a hardcoded `status:'PASS'` and no `detect_stale_runs` (P1-02); the diversity-aware top-K selector is computed but never consumed by deploy (P9-01); the return-sanity guard exists in code but was never applied to the on-disk verdicts it protects (P8-05); cost-drift is wired but its denominator is wrong so it is permanently FIRED (P10-01) and entirely absent for gmgp1-btc (P10-02).

**Top S1 themes (3 confirmed S1):**
1. **Stale/cheap-cost OOS verdicts that contradict the falsification and read green.** The on-file GMGP1-BTC recent-OOS verdict reports PASS at PF 2.149 / **+5,437% return** for a strategy the cost-corrected canary FALSIFIED (P8-01), because the standalone evaluator runs taker-fee-only (slippage 0) with uncapped compounding and **no return-sanity bound** (P8-02). These are the AlphaSeek frictionless-artifact trap, and there is no `superseded_by` mechanism to stop a stale PASS being read as a deploy gate.
2. **OOS test-window leaking into seed selection.** The L1→WF auto-chain promotes the top-3 seeds by **test PF** (`backtest_test/profit_factor`), not val PF — leaking the OOS split into the seed-promotion decision that builds the live ensemble (P6-01), in direct violation of S495 `val_argmax_pf`. **Top S2 themes:**

**Credit where due (S4 positives, verified by mutation/probe):** X2 causal map closed at mid-interval truncation (P2-04, P10-07); LEAK-1 EMA-Z isolation exact (P2-05); PRISM L1 daily lookup causal at the midnight tie (P2-06); start-date trim does not desync the index map (P2-07); SHORT-ACCT debt-free symmetric (P3-09); single-source fee + causal gap-mask (P3-10); DSR formula correct vs MATH-R01 with a strong numeric tripwire (P4-05, P4-06); reward↔PF Spearman computed (P4-04, P5-10); BUG-01/BUG-03 fail-closed in the validator (P5-11, P7-07); paired block-bootstrap sound (P9-09); externalized gates raise-on-missing (P9-10); v2.3 bundle SHA chain fail-closed covering checkpoints AND normalizers (P10-08); the cost_gap detector that correctly KILLED v1 allocator RL + leak check + frozen-baseline uplift discipline (P11-07).
