## Executive Summary

### GATE VERDICT: **PROCEED-WITH-FIXES** (toward continued rung-1 build; **BLOCK on rung-2 / paper-capital** until the confirmed S2 forward-path + gate-calibration findings are resolved)

The rung-1 step-3 build is **engineered well below the waterline and over-claimed above it.** The accounting core — `PaperState.step_bar` as a verbatim, step-by-step-pinned transcription of `MultiAssetAllocatorEnv.step`, including the SHORT-ACCT signed-entry-notional path, the forced-liquidation guard, the F1 dollar-volume slippage, and the v1.1 rates-carry seam — is genuinely strong and is the right foundation (P1-08, P2-08, P3-06, P10-07, P11-07 all confirmed POSITIVE). The headline "parity ≈ 0" is **real, not a frictionless artifact**: on the validated 18-asset ETF universe both sides carry an identical $31,065.69 of real fees to a matching $392,649.88 final equity at net Sharpe 0.441 (P1-07, P3-05, P10/P11 confirmed). **There is NO confirmed S1 finding and NO live capital at risk today.**

But this is a **PRE-CAPITAL gate**, and the audit's central instruction — *find out WHY parity is 0 and whether that 0 is load-bearing or tautological* — resolves against the spec's framing. **There are 14 confirmed S2 findings.** The forward path the soak's entire value proposition rests on (independent live recompute, calendar/scheduler, growing-window assembly) **is the unbuilt step-4 and is exercised by NONE of the rung-1 keystone metrics.**

**The single dominant theme — "parity ≈ 0 is load-bearing for ACCOUNTING, tautological for the FORWARD DATA/WEIGHT path; and the capital gate certifies a *different, lower-Sharpe, higher-drawdown* strategy than the one Fable validated."** This is the X2-coarse-bar-leak lesson in a new guise: a green wrapper around a target that is not the validated one, with the green dominated by code-agreement rather than forward-path correctness.

Five supporting strands, all confirmed:

1. **The shadowed strategy is not the validated strategy (P2-02, P11-01).** The GO edge (net SR 0.601) was struck on **monthly-frozen** weights; the executor shadows `evaluate_linear_core`'s **daily vol-rescale**, which renders the *same* TSMOM core at net SR **0.441** on the 20.4-yr history — a different return stream (the env's gross-cap 3.0 + lev-cap 2.0 clips the falsification's 74.6%-vol book down to ~19.6% vol, changing composition, not just leverage). 0.441 still sits inside the Fable honest band [0.40, 0.60], so this is a *reconcile-and-document* item, not edge destruction — but the soak certifies fidelity to the lower-Sharpe rendering, and that must be stated in the gate decision. (The finder's "6–12% cost erosion / X2-trap" framing was **refuted** — turnover is comparable-to-lower than the monthly path; the real residual is the un-reconciled Sharpe/DD profile.)

2. **The risk kill-switches are calibrated to ~half the shadowed strategy's intrinsic risk (P11-02, P8-04).** The env-path book the executor shadows has a **42.8% historical max DD** and is >20% underwater on 34% of days; running `evaluate_paper_soak_gates` on the strategy's own full history returns **overall FAIL** (DD 42.8% vs kill 20.0%; worst day −9.77% vs halt 4.0%). The 20% DD kill and 4% daily-loss halt were sized for a 10%-vol / 22%-DD book that the env path is **not**. An always-on kill below the strategy's validated drawdown converts a normal drawdown into a forced flatten + a false "executor broken" signal. Separately the `max_gross_exposure: 6.0` kill **cannot trip** (env caps gross at 3.0; comment "matches env.max_gross_exposure" is factually wrong).

3. **The capital gate can emit PASS before its own declared horizon, and several HARD gates cannot fail (P8-01, P8-02, P8-04, P8-07, P10-01, P3-01, P11-04).** `min_soak_calendar_days: 90` and `min_rebalances_observed: 3` have **zero Python consumers** — a 10-trading-day / 0-rebalance trajectory returns `overall_status='PASS'`, the documented capital-promotion trigger. `missed_rebalances` derives its own "expected" calendar from the processed bars (always 0). `weight_l1_drift` and `cost_drift_ratio` are byte-identically 0.0 / 1.0 because the live replay consumes the **oracle's own weights** and a copy of the env's own cost formula. The verdict routing is **fail-OPEN** for UNKNOWN in a HARD group. So of the four PRIMARY parity gates, only `daily_return_te_bps` (accounting half) and the equity allclose are load-bearing in rung-1; the headline weight/cost/missed-rebalance trio is structurally non-binding.

4. **The LEAK-2 guards are partly vacuous, and a latent month-end-boundary landmine is armed for step-4 (P2-01, P2-04, P3-07).** `monthly_rebal_conviction` unconditionally flags the in-progress final bar of any truncated window as a month-end, so its `[-1]` is the partial-month raw daily tail. The env path **dodges this today** (it lags one bar, and `test_forward_prefix_consistency` passes), but the `w[-1]` docstrings invite the step-4 scheduler to read exactly the contaminated tail — an ADR-2 "daily is a different strategy" / X2-class trap. The causality tripwire is **2-of-3 vacuous**: a realistic intra-month forward leak is caught at only one of its three parametrized cutoffs (the others land on month-ends and absorb the leak).

5. **Declared-but-not-wired invariants overstate coverage (P1-03, P1-05, P1-06, P2-06, P3-03, P8-08, P11-05).** The spec checklist marks PF-XCHECK, the DATA-CLEAN stale-print scan, and the manifest `status=PASS` as satisfied, but PF-XCHECK has no consumer in `finrl_pro_ds/paper`, `_clean_wide` never calls `validate_ohlcv`/`detect_stale_runs` (the exact detector for the gmgp1-gold flat-OHLC class that voided "directional RL almost worked on gold"), and `manifest['status']='PASS'` is a hardcoded literal. The cache-hit branch keys only on the asset superset and **ignores `date_max`**, so a scheduled live loader would silently freeze on a 9-day-stale cache.

**Honest sim-to-live gaps rung-1 structurally CANNOT catch (all step-4 work):** (a) live data-fetch / calendar / scheduler-date errors and the stale-cache freeze (P1-02); (b) the open-vs-close execution-timing gap — `fill_reference` is a decorative config key with no consumer, `FillResult.fill_prices` is computed and never read (P3-03); (c) the gate-triggered DD-flatten — the live executor flattens at 20% DD while the oracle/replay trade to the end, so parity is *undefined* once a real soak hits the kill, and the harness currently **crashes** (unhandled `AssertionError`) instead of degrading when the env circuit breaker fires (P10-03); (d) the production signal-compute path (`cross_asset_signals.compute` on a growing window) — fixtures feed precomputed conviction, so the LEAK-2 tripwire proves env-accounting causality *given* causal inputs, not the live signal path (Fable-covered, P8-09); (e) `cost_drift_ratio` only becomes informative at rung-2 with real IB fills (one-sided gate, passes an under-/non-trading book, P8-06).

**Credit where due (preserve):** the step-by-step env-pin and forced-liquidation tests; the line-for-line cost-formula transcription with matching epsilons; `test_forward_prefix_consistency` (the correct shape for the step-4 keystone); yaml-sourced thresholds with correct HARD/REVIEW routing; atomic tmp+replace serialization; the F1 dollar-volume participation alignment; and the recognition (in the spec) that a monthly low-frequency core makes short-soak Sharpe statistically meaningless — execution-parity-as-primary is the methodologically correct choice *for the right target*.

**Bottom line:** Continue the rung-1 build — the foundation is sound. **Do not read a green rung-1 soak as forward-path validation, and do not promote to rung-2 / paper-capital** until: the shadowed-strategy Sharpe/DD reconciliation is documented (or the portfolio-vol overlay added), the risk kill-switches are recalibrated to the env path, the horizon gates are wired, the month-end calendar is made truncation-robust + independent, and the docstrings/spec are corrected to scope parity to accounting. None is an emergency; all are pre-capital blockers.
