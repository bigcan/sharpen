## 1. Executive summary

**The operator's question — "is 0-PROMISING market truth or machine artifact?" — has a measured answer: artifact. The funnel, as configured, has power ≈ 0.00 against every realistic alpha (ΔSR 0.3–0.5), so its lifetime record of zero discoveries carries almost no information about whether real alphas were proposed.**

The dominant cross-cutting fault is **compound conservatism around a mis-calibrated significance instrument**, compounded by **operational fictions** — declared safeguards that have no wired consumer. Concretely:

1. **The gate cannot see realistic alphas — measured.** Crucible's own calibration (E2/MDE sweep) puts the minimum detectable marginal ΔSR at 80% power at **1.40–3.63** across substrate lengths, versus a realistic true-alpha band of 0.3–0.5, where measured power is **0.00 at every length**. These are *upper bounds* (full-panel gate, `gen_n_eff=50`; the shipped binding gate re-tests on the 25% holdout at file-drawer N in the hundreds-to-thousands).
2. **The flagship run made it worse than the calibration assumes.** The only US real-mode tick ever mined was invoked with `start=2024-07-02` — a ~504-bar substrate, *below the smallest calibration point* (T=756, MDE 3.63). Nothing records or guards substrate power at invocation (`catalog.db` date ranges ✓✓; script default is 2008-01-01 — the short window was an unguarded invocation accident).
3. **Five serially-ANDed legs, applied twice.** E1 shows `dsr` and `marginal_t` are *each individually* ~total null filters (0.0% and 0.17% null pass) — stacking both buys ~nothing in false-positive protection and ~squares the false-negative rate; the full 5-leg gate must then pass **again** on the holdout alone (t≥3.0 on 189–1,011 bars ⇒ annualized marginal SR ≈ 1.5–3.6). The t=3.0 HLZ hurdle was calibrated for ~50 years of monthly factor data; transplanted to quarter-length daily windows it is 2–4× harsher than intended.
4. **The book-level DSR bar seals whole substrates.** `dsr_aug≥0.90` scores the *absolute augmented book*; taiwan's base book is a single TSMOM sleeve, so **no candidate of any quality can pass there** — 236 of 254 lifetime trials ran on sealed substrates (max observed dsr 0.049 taiwan vs 0.674 cross-asset).
5. **Pre-registration is never cashed.** The system's deepest structural miss: a hash-locked, ex-ante hypothesis is statistically entitled to a plain α-level test — that is the entire scientific point of pre-registration — yet pre-registered specs are deflated against the GP's own ~10³-genome file-drawer exactly like evolved offspring, while the LORD++ online-FDR layer that should meter per-hypothesis α **gates nothing** (zero production consumers of `next_level`/`is_starved`; wealth 96% spent on taiwan_v2 with no effect on anything).
6. **One-shot hypothesis burn.** Dedup is a formula-only hash with no substrate/length dimension: a hypothesis tried once — typically at minimum power, on 40-bar-deep TAIFEX slots or a 504-bar panel — is **permanently barred from re-test** as data accrues. Combined with (1), every early trial destroys its hypothesis at near-zero detection probability. True re-discovery is structurally impossible within a ledger.
7. **The forward lockbox — the design's declared arbiter of truth — is a statistical coin flip with a broken last mile.** 63 forward bars vs an annualized-Sharpe floor of 0.30, where the estimator SE ≈ 2.0: P(clear | true ΔSR 0.4) ≈ 52% vs P(clear | null) ≈ 44%, and REJECTED is terminal forever. And if anything ever CLEARED, no wired path notifies a human (governance scan exists only as a manual script nothing schedules).
8. **Several declared safeguards are fictional.** The killed-family firewall ("~20 NO-GOs the agent must not rediscover") was never seeded and has no writer — it is empty in every DB. Real-mode verdicts have **no reproduce path** (recipes are written only for synthetic+library runs). "Continuous nightly operation" is manual and episodic, and multi-night runs stamp future-dated `proposal_ts` (CR-2 timestamps are fiction for those ticks).

**What is NOT broken:** the falsification machinery itself is real. E1 null calibration is clean (0 false PROMISING / 2,349 noise genomes); the anti-oracle moat (CRU-2) verifies leak-free at code level with allow-list + leak tests; the as-of-join PIT tripwire is implemented, wired in the hot path, and negative-tested; the `delta_p05_min` repair landed *before* the CRU-1 freeze with a documented gate-change protocol; cost realism (5× validated anchor) is in the metric; synthetic/real state is cleanly separated; CRU-1 pinning holds in the actual artifacts. **Crucible is an excellent falsifier pointed at an impossible detection problem, with its payoff paths unwired.**

**Bottom line:** the machine is honest but deaf, and if it ever heard something it would not tell you. Every past "0 PROMISING" reads as "no alpha of ΔSR ≳ 1.4–3.6 existed in 164 hypotheses" — a statement that was never in doubt. §5 gives the ranked fix roadmap: the highest-leverage no-invariant change is **enable the cohort/weak-signal ensemble gate**; the deepest structural fix is **cash the pre-registration** (full-panel, α-metered tests for prereg'd specs, making the decorative FDR layer the real arbiter); the cheapest correctness fixes are the wiring bugs (snapshot-before-mine, verdict write-back, ledger upsert clobber, governance epilogue, real timestamps, substrate power stamp).

## 3. The decision math (pillar C10)

From `results/crucible_calibration/calibration_mde_sweep.json` (30 seeds × 9 β × 4 lengths; drives the SHIPPED gate `evolve()→combination_fitness.passes_gate`):

| Substrate T (bars) | Holdout bars | Power @ ΔSR≈0.5 | Power @ ΔSR≈0.8 | Power @ ΔSR≈1.4 | MDE @ 80% power |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 756 | 189 | 0.00 | 0.00 (@0.72) | 0.00 | **3.63** |
| 1512 | 378 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.10 (@1.37) | **2.85** |
| 2782 (taiwan_v2) | 696 | 0.00 | 0.03 (@0.81) | 0.43 (@1.39) | **1.94** |
| 4044 (cross_asset class) | 1011 | 0.00 (@0.52) | 0.03 (@0.82) | 0.80 (@1.40) | **1.40** |
| **~504 (cross_asset as actually mined)** | ~126 | — | — | — | **> 3.63 (off-grid)** |

Caveats making these **optimistic**: full-panel gate (binding gate is the 25% holdout re-pass), `gate_probe_gen_n_eff=50` (real ticks deflate against the full `gen_n_total`), clean synthetic injection.

**Expected discoveries.** Lifetime charged tests = 8 + 32 + 124 = 164 (`orchestrator.db::fdr_state`). With a generous 3% true-alpha base rate and pooled power ≤ ~1% in the realistic band (0 detections in every ≤0.52 cell; pooled CP-95 ≈ 1–2%): expected lifetime discoveries ≈ **0.05**. Even a *perfect* proposer (100% base rate) expects ≈ 1.6. **The record cannot distinguish a brilliant proposer from a broken one — the experiment as run is uninformative.**

**The arithmetic wall.** For a *selected* candidate, 80% power at t≥2.0 needs true t ≈ 2.84 ⇒ N ≈ (2.84/(ΔSR/√252))². At ΔSR=0.5 that is ≈ **8,100 independent daily holdout bars (~32 years; ~128 years of substrate at holdout_frac 0.25)**. No admissible threshold fixes this (t≈0.57 would be needed at current N ⇒ FPR ≈ 28%). What follows mathematically:

1. Per-candidate significance testing on daily data is the wrong instrument for ΔSR ≤ 0.5. The instrument must change: **aggregate** (ensembles), **lengthen** (history), **widen** (breadth), **accumulate forward** (lockbox with a coherent criterion), or **meter honest α to pre-registered hypotheses on the full panel**.
2. Prereg-cashing arithmetic: a hash-locked spec tested one-sided at α_t≈0.02 on the FULL T=4044 panel ⇒ MDE(80%) ≈ (2.05+0.84)/√4044·√252 ≈ **0.72**; at T≈8000 (Stooq/FRED decades) ≈ **0.51**. With cohort ensembles (K uncorrelated ΔSR-0.3 overlays ⇒ ≈0.3√K; K=9 ⇒ 0.9) the realistic band becomes reachable **with no relaxation of false-positive control**.
3. The lockbox needs the same math: at 63 forward bars the SR estimator SE ≈ √(252/63) ≈ 2.0 ⇒ its current criterion discriminates 52% vs 44% (see C8-01). At 252 forward bars SE ≈ 1.0 — still weak; the criterion should be a lower-confidence-bound or t-test with a pre-registered power statement, and horizon extension must be pre-registered (it is sequential testing).
