"Sharpen" and "Crucible", used as names for this software and its alpha-mining platform, are
trademarks of Keng Lee. Neither name is registered.

The [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE) covers the **code**. It does not grant rights to the **names**
(Apache 2.0, §6). This policy says how you may use the names. Its goal is to make sure no one is
misled about who built a piece of software or who stands behind a result.

## You may, without asking

- **Say what you use:** "built with Sharpen", "uses Sharpen's Crucible funnel", "based on Sharpen".
- **Refer to the project** accurately in papers, articles, talks, videos, course material and
  documentation, with a link back to the repository.
- **Name a fork factually** in its README, e.g. "a fork of Sharpen", provided the fork itself
  carries a different name.
- **Keep the names** in unmodified copies you redistribute, and in the `LICENSE`, `NOTICE` and
  copyright headers the Apache License requires you to preserve.

## You may not, without written permission

- **Name a product, service, fund, strategy or company** "Sharpen" or "Crucible", or anything
  confusingly similar (e.g. "Sharpen Pro", "SharpenAI", "Crucible Alpha").
- **Distribute a modified version under either name.** A modified fork must be renamed.
- **Imply endorsement, partnership or certification**, e.g. "Sharpen-certified", "Sharpen-verified
  strategy", "audited by Sharpen", or any suggestion that the author reviewed, validated or backs
  your strategy, signals, results or returns.
- **Use the names in marketing an investment product or service.** This includes fund documents,
  investor decks and track-record claims. Sharpen's verdicts are the output of software you ran,
  not an opinion from its author.
- **Register a domain, social-media handle or package name** that uses either name for a
  project other than this one.

## Research results

You may report that a strategy passed or failed Sharpen's gates, provided you also state the
version or commit, the configuration, and that you ran the evaluation yourself. Do not describe a
result as endorsed by the Sharpen project.

## Permission and questions

For a use not covered here, or for commercial licensing of the names, contact the maintainer
through [github.com/bigcan](https://github.com/bigcan). If you're unsure whether a use is fine,
ask; the answer is usually yes when the use is honest about where the software came from.
